# Hearthwood — Little village, big heart

A cozy 3D village life-sim that turns into a gentle night-survival game. Open `index.html` in a modern browser with an internet connection (Three.js loads from jsDelivr).

## Play

- **Move:** virtual joystick on touch screens, or WASD / arrow keys.
- **Gather / talk / fish:** walk near an item, pond, or neighbor and tap **Gather** (or press **E**).
- **Craft:** tap **Craft** (or press **C**) to make barricades, benches, and lanterns.
- **Build:** craft a barricade, then tap **Build** (or press **B**) to place one in front of you.
- **Defend:** tap **Swing** (or press **Q**) to knock back nearby zombies after dark.

Gather during the 100-second day, then defend the village through the 78-second night. Survive until sunrise for a coin bonus and a fresh day.
